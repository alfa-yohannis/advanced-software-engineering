Tardigrades, also known as water bears, are tiny animals that live in water, soil, moss, and even the deep sea. Most are less than one millimeter long, and they have eight stubby legs with little claws.

Tardigrades are famous for surviving extreme conditions. When their environment dries out, many curl into a dormant state called a tun. Their metabolism slows dramatically, allowing them to survive severe dehydration and even exposure to the vacuum of space.

They aren't invincible, though. They need water to be active, grow, and reproduce, and extreme heat can kill them.
